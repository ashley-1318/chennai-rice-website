His story

From a family trade to an integrated mill.

2013 Chennai Rice Industries

He incorporated the company with one conviction: control every stage, from the paddy in the field to the pack on the shelf.

Today 1,000+ tonnes a day

Three production units, six warehouses across Tamil Nadu and a procurement network reaching five states.
